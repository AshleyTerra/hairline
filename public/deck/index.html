The brief

Replace MySalon with something the team will actually use.

  • Cover the whole salon operation, but keep it simple — no overkill.
  • Focus on invoicing, client history, pricing, stock, and staff.
  • Win buy-in from reception, stylists and the owner.
  • Build it properly — this could be a product for other salons.
THE TEST WE SET OURSELVES

The new till must be faster than the old one.

Every study of salon software adoption says the same thing: if checkout slows down, the team stops using it, whatever else the system can do.

08

The screen that decides everything

The till, with a stopwatch on it.

  • One search finds a client, a service, or a scanned barcode.
  • Big tappable tiles — laid out for a counter, not a desk.
  • The balance owing is the largest thing on screen, with a keypad beneath it.
  • Split payments, tips per stylist, and a timer that keeps the 30-second target honest.
  • Keeps working when the internet drops; syncs when it returns.
HOW WE KNOW THE MATHS IS RIGHT

18 automated tests on the money logic

VAT, quantities, percentage discounts, split payments, change on cash, voucher limits and tips are each tested independently of the screen — so the arithmetic is proven, not hoped for.

The salon's most valuable asset

Every client, every visit, instantly.

  • Search by name or phone; open a full visit timeline in one click.

Buy-in by design

Three people, three different systems.

OWNER

The business, on your phone

Takings so far today, month against last, top stylists, stock value, retail share, and what needs attention — without phoning the salon.

RECEPTION

A faster front desk

Till, clients, diary, stock and the familiar denomination cash-up. No profit reports, no staff pay — less to learn, less to get wrong.

STYLIST

Their own numbers

Today's clients, month against target, tips earned, retail share. Their figures, on their own phone — the difference between a system done to the team and for them.

11

Going live without risk

Nothing switches over until the numbers match.

  1. Build the heart — till, cash-up, clients, loaded with migrated data. 6–8 weeks
  2. Shadow run — the new till runs alongside MySalon; daily totals must match to the cent. 2 weeks
  3. Go live — invoicing and cash-up cut over; MySalon becomes read-only. 1 weekend
  4. Stock & team — stock control, orders, stock takes, staff portfolios, time clock. +4–6 weeks
  5. Messaging & diary — SMS and WhatsApp templates, the light diary, stylist phone views. +3–4 weeks

Stylist dashboards arrive only after reception already trusts the till.

12

How we'll know it worked

Five things, all of them measurable.

  • A routine sale rung up in 30 seconds or less; cash-up done in five minutes.
  • Shadow-run totals match MySalon to the cent, two weeks running.
  • Reception says they prefer the new till within a month.
  • The owner checks the day's takings on their phone, not by phoning in.
  • Zero data loss — every historic visit still findable.
